## Things to know before you buy

A few non-obvious points that catch new BandwagonHost users off guard:

**Self-managed only.** BandwagonHost explicitly markets itself as self-managed to keep prices down. There's no cPanel, no managed support, no "we'll fix your nginx config for you." You get root via KiwiVM (their in-house control panel), SSH, and an emergency console. If you've never administered a Linux box before, factor in the learning curve or plan to use a control panel like aaPanel, CloudPanel, or HestiaCP on top.

**30-day refund, but only on first purchase.** The refund policy covers your first invoice within 30 days. Renewals are non-refundable. Apply the promo code before paying — you can't retroactively claim the discount on a completed order.

**Free location migration, but not between plan tiers.** You can move a VPS between datacenters in the same product line (e.g., between DC6 CN2 GIA-E and DC9 CN2 GIA, or between Los Angeles and Amsterdam on the basic KVM line) for free, without data loss, through KiwiVM. You cannot migrate from a basic KVM plan to a CN2 GIA-E plan without upgrading the product. So pick the right product line at sign-up.

**Renewal price = the price you paid, not the current list price.** Because the promo code is recurring, your renewal invoice will use the same discounted price as your first invoice. If BandwagonHost raises list prices later, your locked-in discounted rate is still based on the list price at the time of your first order — at least, that's been the historical behavior. Don't treat this as a guarantee, but it's how it has worked for the past several years.

**Stock-based availability on popular plans.** Hong Kong and Tokyo plans, and the cheaper CN2 GIA-E tiers, sometimes show as out of stock. If the plan you want isn't available, check back in a few days — BandwagonHost restocks regularly but doesn't announce restock schedules.

**OS templates.** All plans support AlmaLinux, RockyLinux, CentOS, Debian, Ubuntu, CentOS Stream, and Fedora out of the box. Custom ISOs can be added on request. KiwiVM also handles snapshots, rDNS (PTR) records, and an API for automation.
